About

I design for how people actually live.

I studied product and industrial design at Lovely Professional University (Bachelor of Design, 2020). Most of my work is biomedical products, footwear and furniture: a healing shoe for diabetic patients, a hand-therapy peg board, an X-ray positioning pillow, and furniture that folds away when you don't need it.

I like starting from a real problem someone has, building a physical model early, and refining the details until the product is simple to use.
