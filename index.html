2026Alerts · UX Designer II
Jan–May 2026 · Logistics SaaS · Shipped

Shipment notifications

Customers waited for update emails that admins wrote by hand. I designed a three-level system (admin defaults, customer versions, personal settings) so 249 shipment events alert the right people on their own.

  • Found a middle ground with engineering. Engineers pushed back on my first idea, a yes/no popup. I proposed a Recipients section instead: one place for users to choose, and clean data for engineers.
  • Proposed the next step myself. After launch I designed a "who gets what" view for the next release, for a need v1 didn't solve.
Owned mistakeMy first version let alerts reach every company under a customer. A customer demo caught it. See what I changed →
